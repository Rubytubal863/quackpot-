REM Title: Screen Barrel Roll
REM Description: flips the screen 180 for a few seconds, then rights it.
REM Target OS: Windows (Intel iGPU rotation hotkeys only)
REM Novelty: a timed 180 flip on intel gpu hotkeys with auto-revert, not the repo's Resolution_Prank.
REM Revert: auto. ctrl+alt+up restores it after the delay. no-op on non-intel gpus.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let HID enumerate before any keystrokes
DELAY 1200

REM Intel Graphics rotation hotkey: rotate the display 180 degrees (upside-down).
REM Does nothing on AMD/NVIDIA-only systems, or if driver hot keys are disabled.
CTRL ALT DOWNARROW

REM hold the flip for a few seconds
DELAY 4000

REM Intel Graphics rotation hotkey: rotate back to 0 degrees (normal).
CTRL ALT UPARROW
