REM Title: Volume Serenade
REM Description: ramps the volume up in steps with a ping at each, then restores the exact original level.
REM Target OS: macOS
REM Novelty: reads the current output volume and restores it exactly, no extra tools.
REM Revert: auto, it restores the saved level at the end.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Give the HID keyboard time to enumerate
DELAY 1200

REM Open Spotlight and launch Terminal
GUI SPACE
DELAY 500
STRINGLN terminal
REM Wait for Terminal to come up
DELAY 1500

REM One line (bash): $OLD captures the current output volume via osascript, we ramp 20->80 playing Ping.aiff at each step, then restore $OLD exactly. The $ vars are shell variables, expanded on the host, not Ducky variables.
STRINGLN OLD=$(osascript -e 'output volume of (get volume settings)'); for v in 20 40 60 80; do osascript -e "set volume output volume $v"; afplay /System/Library/Sounds/Ping.aiff; done; osascript -e "set volume output volume $OLD"

REM Give the serenade time to play and restore before closing the shell
DELAY 6000
STRINGLN exit
