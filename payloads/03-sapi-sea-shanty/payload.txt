REM Title: SAPI Sea Shanty
REM Description: sings a short duck sea shanty out loud through the built-in sapi voice.
REM Target OS: Windows
REM Novelty: a windows sapi shanty with its own lyrics; the repo's talkers are macos (MacTalk) or a generic talking duck.
REM Revert: none, it's audio.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
DELAY 1200

REM Open Run, then speak via the SAPI.SpVoice COM object. Rate=-1 slows it for a sing-song feel.
REM Console runs visibly (no stealth): it flashes open, speaks, exits.
GUI r
DELAY 500
STRINGLN powershell -c "$v=New-Object -ComObject SAPI.SpVoice;$v.Rate=-1;$v.Speak('Oh we sail the seven puddles, quack quack quack');$v.Speak('a rubber ducky captain, and it never quacks back')"
