REM Title: Notify-Send Duck Parade
REM Description: fires a few friendly desktop notifications, then they expire.
REM Target OS: Linux (needs notify-send / libnotify)
REM Novelty: a libnotify notification parade, nothing like it in the repo.
REM Revert: auto, the notifications expire on their own.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the HID keyboard interface enumerate before typing
DELAY 1200

REM Open the default terminal (GNOME and most desktops: Ctrl+Alt+T)
CTRL ALT t
REM Give the terminal window time to spawn and take focus
DELAY 1200

REM Fire four notifications, one per second; each auto-expires from the notification daemon
STRINGLN for m in "a wild duck appears" "it means well" "it has been reversed already" "quack"; do notify-send "Quackpot" "$m"; sleep 1; done
