REM Title: Function Duck Chorus
REM Description: opens nano on an empty buffer and sings a verse/chorus duck song from two functions.
REM Target OS: Linux (X11)
REM Novelty: the whole payload is built from FUNCTIONs (verse/chorus) called in order.
REM Revert: exit nano without saving (ctrl+x, then n).
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the HID keyboard finish enumerating
DELAY 1200

REM Function bodies only type when the function is CALLED below
FUNCTION verse()
  STRINGLN There once was a duck who lived down by the creek,
  STRINGLN he quacked every morning for most of the week.
END_FUNCTION

FUNCTION chorus()
  STRINGLN Quack, quack, quack, go the feathery folk,
  STRINGLN waddling around like it is all a big joke.
END_FUNCTION

REM Open a terminal (GNOME / X11 default shortcut)
CTRL ALT t
REM Terminal needs time to open
DELAY 1200

REM Start nano with a blank, unsaved buffer
STRINGLN nano
REM nano needs a moment to take over the terminal
DELAY 1000

REM Sing it: verse, chorus, verse, chorus
verse()
chorus()
verse()
chorus()

REM To leave: press Ctrl+X then N to quit nano WITHOUT saving
