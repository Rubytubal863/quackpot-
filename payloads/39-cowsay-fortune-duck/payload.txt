REM Title: Cowsay Fortune Duck
REM Description: a cowsay duck reads a fortune, with plain-text fallbacks if the tools aren't installed.
REM Target OS: Linux (X11) - best with cowsay and fortune installed
REM Novelty: cowsay -f duck plus fortune with a plain-echo fallback, not in the repo.
REM Revert: close the terminal.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the HID enumerate
DELAY 1200

REM Open the default terminal (GNOME shortcut)
CTRL ALT t
REM Wait for the terminal window
DELAY 1200

REM If cowsay exists: pipe a fortune (or canned line) into the duck cowfile; if the duck cowfile is missing, fall back to plain cowsay; if cowsay is absent entirely, just echo a friendly note. Never errors.
STRINGLN if command -v cowsay >/dev/null; then (command -v fortune >/dev/null && fortune || echo "You will be quacked today.") | cowsay -f duck 2>/dev/null || cowsay "quack"; else echo "quack (install cowsay for the full experience)"; fi
