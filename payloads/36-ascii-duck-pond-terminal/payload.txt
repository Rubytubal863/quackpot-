REM Title: ASCII Duck Pond
REM Description: prints an ascii duck pond into the terminal.
REM Target OS: Linux (X11) - needs a terminal that opens on CTRL ALT t (GNOME default)
REM Novelty: a heredoc pond scene, not in the repo.
REM Revert: close the terminal.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the HID enumerate before we send anything
DELAY 1200

REM Open the default terminal (GNOME shortcut)
CTRL ALT t
REM Wait for the terminal window to appear
DELAY 1200

REM Single-quoted heredoc tag ('POND') means the shell expands NOTHING inside - no globbing of ~, no (), <, backslashes stay literal
STRINGLN cat <<'POND'
STRINGLN    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
STRINGLN    ~    __(.)<  quack!     ~
STRINGLN    ~    \___)              ~
STRINGLN    ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
STRINGLN   |//|   |//|      |\\|  |\\|
STRINGLN        a serene little pond
REM The terminator on its own line ends the heredoc and cat flushes the art
STRINGLN POND
