REM Title: Mac Emoji Picker Parade
REM Description: opens textedit and walks emoji in through the character viewer.
REM Target OS: macOS
REM Novelty: uses the macos emoji picker (ctrl+cmd+space).
REM Revert: close textedit without saving.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Give the HID keyboard time to enumerate
DELAY 1200

REM Open Spotlight and launch TextEdit
GUI SPACE
DELAY 500
STRINGLN textedit
REM Wait for TextEdit to come up
DELAY 1500

REM New blank document (Cmd+N)
GUI n
DELAY 500

REM CTRL GUI SPACE opens the Character Viewer (emoji picker). Type the name to filter, ENTER inserts the top match.

REM duck
CTRL GUI SPACE
DELAY 400
STRING duck
DELAY 400
ENTER
DELAY 200

REM frog
CTRL GUI SPACE
DELAY 400
STRING frog
DELAY 400
ENTER
DELAY 200

CTRL GUI SPACE
DELAY 400
STRING cat
DELAY 400
ENTER
DELAY 200

CTRL GUI SPACE
DELAY 400
