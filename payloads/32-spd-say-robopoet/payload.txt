REM Title: spd-say Robo-Poet
REM Description: reads a short robotic ode to rubber ducks out loud.
REM Target OS: Linux (needs speech-dispatcher for spd-say, or espeak as fallback)
REM Novelty: linux tts via spd-say with an espeak fallback, not the macos talkers.
REM Revert: none, audio only.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the HID keyboard interface enumerate before typing
DELAY 1200

REM Open the default terminal (GNOME and most desktops: Ctrl+Alt+T)
CTRL ALT t
REM Give the terminal window time to spawn and take focus
DELAY 1200

REM If spd-say exists, recite the ode; otherwise fall back to espeak saying 'quack'
STRINGLN command -v spd-say >/dev/null && spd-say -t male3 "Beep. I am a duck. Boop. I float. Beep. Squeeze me. Boop. I squeak." || espeak "quack"
