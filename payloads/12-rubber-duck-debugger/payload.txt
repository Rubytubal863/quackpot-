REM Title: Rubber Duck Debugger
REM Description: the duck debugs your code by asking the usual rubber-duck questions, then shouts the punchline.
REM Target OS: Windows
REM Novelty: on-theme rubber-duck-debugging, and the punchline uses HOLD SHIFT to shout.
REM Revert: close notepad without saving.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the host enumerate the HID keyboard before typing.
DELAY 1200

REM Open the Run dialog and launch Notepad.
GUI r
REM Wait for the Run dialog to appear.
DELAY 500
STRINGLN notepad
REM Wait for Notepad to be focused and ready for input.
DELAY 1200

STRINGLN Hi. I am your rubber duck. Let's debug this together.
DELAY 300
STRINGLN Walk me through it line by line.
DELAY 300
STRINGLN Now... what did you EXPECT to happen?
DELAY 300
STRINGLN And what actually happened?
DELAY 300
STRINGLN Are you sure that variable is what you think it is?
DELAY 300

REM Punchline: hold SHIFT so every letter is typed uppercase (shouted).
HOLD SHIFT
STRING have you tried turning your assumptions off and on again
RELEASE SHIFT
ENTER
