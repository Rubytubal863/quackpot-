REM Title: Beep Symphony
REM Description: plays a six-note phrase on the system beep. no volume change, no files.
REM Target OS: Windows
REM Novelty: a composed [console]::beep tune, self-contained, doesn't touch audio settings like SoundChangeDuck.
REM Revert: none, sound only.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
DELAY 1200

REM Open Run and play a short phrase via [console]::beep(freq_hz, duration_ms).
REM Console runs visibly (no stealth): it flashes open, beeps, exits. No settings are touched.
GUI r
DELAY 500
STRINGLN powershell -c "[console]::beep(523,180);[console]::beep(587,180);[console]::beep(659,180);[console]::beep(523,180);[console]::beep(659,320);[console]::beep(784,400)"
