REM Title: Mission Control Mambo
REM Description: runs a short mission-control and show-desktop dance.
REM Target OS: macOS
REM Novelty: choreographed mission-control moves, not in the repo.
REM Revert: auto, ends back on the normal desktop.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the HID keyboard enumerate before the first chord
DELAY 1200

REM All of these are default Mission Control keyboard shortcuts.

REM Up into Mission Control (Control + Up)
CTRL UPARROW
DELAY 900

REM Back down, out of Mission Control (Control + Down)
CTRL DOWNARROW
DELAY 700

REM Slide one space to the right, then back left (Control + Left/Right).
REM If no adjacent space exists this is simply a no-op - harmless either way.
CTRL RIGHTARROW
DELAY 700
CTRL LEFTARROW
DELAY 700

REM Show Desktop (Command + F3) - fly the windows out to the edges
GUI F3
DELAY 900

REM Command + F3 again brings every window back where it was
GUI F3
