REM Title: Say Beatbox
REM Description: makes the mac beatbox boots-and-cats through say.
REM Target OS: macOS
REM Novelty: tts beatbox, a different use of say.
REM Revert: none, audio only.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the HID keyboard enumerate before typing
DELAY 1200

REM Open Terminal via Spotlight (Command+Space)
GUI SPACE
DELAY 500
STRINGLN terminal
REM Give Terminal a moment to launch and focus
DELAY 1500

REM -r 210 is a fast, beat-friendly words-per-minute rate
STRINGLN say -r 210 "boots and cats and boots and cats and boots and cats and quack"

REM Close the shell
STRINGLN exit
