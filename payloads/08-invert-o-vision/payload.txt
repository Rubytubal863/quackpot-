REM Title: Invert-O-Vision
REM Description: inverts the screen colours through magnifier for a few seconds, then restores.
REM Target OS: Windows
REM Novelty: magnifier colour-invert (ctrl+alt+i) as a timed toggle; no windows invert payload exists.
REM Revert: auto. GUI ESCAPE closes magnifier and undoes the invert and zoom.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let HID enumerate before any keystrokes
DELAY 1200

REM Win + '+' launches Magnifier (opens at 200% zoom)
GUI +
DELAY 600

REM Ctrl+Alt+I toggles Magnifier's full-screen colour inversion (photo-negative)
CTRL ALT i
DELAY 4000

REM Win + Esc closes Magnifier, restoring colours and zoom in one shot
GUI ESCAPE
