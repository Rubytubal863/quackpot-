REM Title: Magnifier Disco
REM Description: strobes the windows magnifier in and out on a beat, then closes it.
REM Target OS: Windows
REM Novelty: a rhythmic zoom instead of the one-shot resolution/zoom prank (repo's Resolution_Prank).
REM Revert: auto. GUI ESCAPE closes magnifier and resets zoom to 100%.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Give the HID a moment to enumerate on the host
DELAY 1200

REM The FIRST plus chord both LAUNCHES Magnifier and zooms it to 200%.
REM GUI + is the plus chord, same literal-symbol form as browser CTRL +
GUI +
DELAY 400

REM Strobe: alternate zoom OUT (GUI -, minus chord) and zoom IN (GUI +) on a beat
GUI -
DELAY 350
GUI +
DELAY 350
GUI -
DELAY 350
GUI +
DELAY 350
GUI -
DELAY 350
GUI +
DELAY 350
GUI -
DELAY 350
GUI +
DELAY 350

REM GUI ESCAPE closes Magnifier and snaps the zoom back to 100%
GUI ESCAPE
