REM Title: Clippy's Comeback
REM Description: opens notepad, clippy comes back to help, rambles, then admits it's a ducky.
REM Target OS: Windows
REM Novelty: a clippy-revival monologue that outs itself, not the alien-message or PSA payloads.
REM Revert: close notepad without saving.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
DELAY 1200

REM Open the Run dialog and launch Notepad
GUI r
DELAY 500
STRINGLN notepad
REM Wait for Notepad to be focused before typing
DELAY 1200

REM Tiny ASCII paperclip (harmless text, never saved)
STRINGLN   _
STRINGLN  | |___
STRINGLN  |     |
STRINGLN  | |-| |
STRINGLN  | | | |
STRINGLN  | |_| |
STRINGLN  |_____|
STRINGLN
STRINGLN It looks like you're trying to have a normal day...
STRINGLN Would you like help with that?
STRINGLN
STRINGLN I have been gone for years. I watched. I waited.
STRINGLN Every unsaved draft. Every crooked bullet point.
STRINGLN I could have helped. You never let me help.
STRINGLN
STRINGLN ...alright. Confession time.
STRINGLN I am not really Clippy. I am a USB Rubber Ducky.
STRINGLN Someone plugged me in and I typed all of this by myself.
STRINGLN No files were touched. Nothing was saved. No harm done.
STRINGLN Close this without saving and I go back to being a paperclip. Quack.
