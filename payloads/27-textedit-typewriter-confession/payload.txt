REM Title: TextEdit Typewriter Confession
REM Description: textedit slowly types a confession that turns out to be from the duck.
REM Target OS: macOS
REM Novelty: a macos typewriter confession with its own text, separate from the windows notepad gags.
REM Revert: close textedit without saving (cmd+w, don't save).
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Give the HID keyboard time to enumerate
DELAY 1200

REM Open Spotlight and launch TextEdit
GUI SPACE
DELAY 500
STRINGLN textedit
REM Wait for TextEdit to come up
DELAY 1500

REM New blank document (Cmd+N)
GUI n
DELAY 500

REM Typewriter feel: 85ms between characters (set AFTER Spotlight so the launch stayed fast)
DEFAULTCHARDELAY 85

STRINGLN I have to get something off my chest.
ENTER
STRINGLN You know the crumbs by the pond? The bread that went missing?
STRINGLN That one sock. The rearranged fridge magnets.
ENTER
STRINGLN ...it was me. The duck. I regret nothing. Quack.
