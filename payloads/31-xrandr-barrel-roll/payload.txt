REM Title: xrandr Barrel Roll
REM Description: flips the x11 display 180 for five seconds, then rights it.
REM Target OS: Linux (X11), no-op or harmless error under Wayland
REM Novelty: an xrandr flip with auto-revert, the linux counterpart to the windows/mac flips.
REM Revert: auto, the one-liner runs xrandr -o normal after the sleep.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the HID keyboard interface enumerate before typing
DELAY 1200

REM Open the default terminal (GNOME and most desktops: Ctrl+Alt+T)
CTRL ALT t
REM Give the terminal window time to spawn and take focus
DELAY 1200

REM Flip the primary output upside-down, hold 5s, then restore to normal orientation
STRINGLN xrandr -o inverted; sleep 5; xrandr -o normal
