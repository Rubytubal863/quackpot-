REM Title: Binary Whisper
REM Description: types a line of binary, pauses, then reveals it spells QUACK.
REM Target OS: Windows
REM Novelty: a binary-reveal, not in the repo.
REM Revert: close notepad without saving.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM let the HID keyboard enumerate before we type anything
DELAY 1000
GUI r
DELAY 500
REM launch Notepad
STRINGLN notepad
REM wait for the Notepad window to be ready for input
DELAY 1200
REM ASCII binary for Q U A C K
STRINGLN 01010001 01010101 01000001 01000011 01001011
REM ominous pause before the reveal
DELAY 2000
STRINGLN (translation: "QUACK")
STRINGLN You have been binary-quacked.
