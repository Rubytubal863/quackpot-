REM Title: Stickies Love Note
REM Description: drops a short wholesome duck sticky on the desktop.
REM Target OS: macOS
REM Novelty: uses the stickies app, not in the repo.
REM Revert: delete the sticky (focus it, cmd+w, delete note).
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Give the HID keyboard time to enumerate
DELAY 1200

REM Open Spotlight and launch Stickies
GUI SPACE
DELAY 500
STRINGLN stickies
REM Wait for Stickies to come up
DELAY 1500

REM New note (Cmd+N)
GUI n
DELAY 500

STRINGLN You are doing great.
STRINGLN - a duck who believes in you
