REM Title: Virtual Desktop Carousel
REM Description: makes three virtual desktops, surfs across them, then closes the ones it made.
REM Target OS: Windows
REM Novelty: a virtual-desktop surf-and-cleanup, not in the repo.
REM Revert: auto. it closes the three desktops it created (win+ctrl+f4 x3) and lands on your original.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let HID enumerate before any keystrokes
DELAY 1200

REM --- Create three new virtual desktops; Win+Ctrl+D both CREATES and SWITCHES to each ---
GUI CTRL d
DELAY 500
GUI CTRL d
DELAY 500
GUI CTRL d
DELAY 500

REM We now sit on the rightmost (3rd created) desktop.
REM --- Tour left across the carousel all the way back to the original (Win+Ctrl+Left) ---
GUI CTRL LEFT
DELAY 400
GUI CTRL LEFT
DELAY 400
GUI CTRL LEFT
DELAY 400

REM --- Surf right again back to the rightmost created desktop (Win+Ctrl+Right) ---
REM Ending on the rightmost is what lets the cleanup below peel desktops off toward the original.
GUI CTRL RIGHT
DELAY 400
GUI CTRL RIGHT
DELAY 400
GUI CTRL RIGHT
DELAY 400

REM --- Tidy up: Win+Ctrl+F4 closes the CURRENT desktop and shifts focus left ---
REM Three closes remove exactly the three we created and leave us on the original.
GUI CTRL F4
DELAY 400
GUI CTRL F4
DELAY 400
GUI CTRL F4
DELAY 400
