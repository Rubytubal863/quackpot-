REM Title: Say Duck Opera
REM Description: performs a tiny duck opera out loud with say.
REM Target OS: macOS
REM Novelty: a say-driven mini opera with its own libretto, not MacTalk.
REM Revert: none, audio only.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let the HID keyboard enumerate before typing
DELAY 1200

REM Open Terminal via Spotlight (Command+Space)
GUI SPACE
DELAY 500
STRINGLN terminal
REM Give Terminal a moment to launch and focus
DELAY 1500

REM Perform the opera. -r 160 sets a stately words-per-minute pace.
REM Swap in a musical voice if it is installed, e.g. add:  -v Bells
STRINGLN say -r 160 "Quaaack. Quack quack quaaaack. A rubber duck, aloooone, upon the poooond."

REM Close the shell cleanly, leaving nothing behind
STRINGLN exit
