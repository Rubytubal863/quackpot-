REM Title: Ducky Traffic Light
REM Description: runs a red-then-green routine on the ducky's own onboard led. types nothing.
REM Target OS: Cross-platform
REM Novelty: pure onboard-led choreography with LED_R/LED_G/LED_OFF, no keystrokes at all.
REM Revert: none, the led turns off at the end.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
DELAY 1000

REM All LED commands below drive the DUCKY'S OWN onboard LED, not the
REM keyboard lock lights. Only three states exist: LED_R (red),
REM LED_G (green), LED_OFF. There is no blue LED.

REM Red: stop, should I really do this?
LED_R
DELAY 1000

REM Deliberating: blink red a few times
LED_OFF
DELAY 200
LED_R
DELAY 200
LED_OFF
DELAY 200
LED_R
DELAY 200
LED_OFF
DELAY 200

REM Green: go for it
LED_G
DELAY 1500

REM All done, LED off
LED_OFF
