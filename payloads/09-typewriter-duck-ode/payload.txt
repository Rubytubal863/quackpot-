REM Title: Typewriter Duck Ode
REM Description: types a short ode to the rubber duck with an old-typewriter cadence.
REM Target OS: Windows (the DEFAULTCHARDELAY + jitter technique is cross-platform)
REM Novelty: uses DEFAULTCHARDELAY plus jitter for the typewriter rhythm.
REM Revert: close notepad without saving.
REM Author: azzie

ATTACKMODE HID
REM Let HID enumerate before any keystrokes
DELAY 1200

REM Open the Run dialog and launch Notepad
GUI r
DELAY 500
STRINGLN notepad
REM Give Notepad a moment to open before typing
DELAY 1200

REM Organic typewriter cadence: a slow base char delay plus random per-key jitter.
REM The jitter is what turns steady robotic typing into a human/typewriter rhythm.
DEFAULTCHARDELAY 90
$_JITTER_ENABLED = TRUE
$_JITTER_MAX = 60

STRINGLN Ode to a Rubber Duck
STRINGLN
STRINGLN Little yellow friend upon my desk,
STRINGLN you never judge, you never quack back.
STRINGLN You float through every stubborn bug,
STRINGLN patient while I talk you through the stack.
STRINGLN And when the fix at last comes clear,
STRINGLN you only smile. You knew it all along.
